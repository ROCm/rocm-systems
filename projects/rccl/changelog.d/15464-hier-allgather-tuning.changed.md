* On gfx1250 DPX, where the hierarchical LSA AllGather (`AllGather_HierLsaST`) can run, symmetric `ncclAllGather` is now selected from 16 MiB total with no upper bound, in eager and graph mode, instead of from 128 KiB to 2 MiB. From 128 KiB to 16 MiB these communicators no longer use symmetric kernels. `RCCL_SYM_K_MIN_BYTES` and `RCCL_SYM_K_MAX_BYTES` still override this window. Communicators where the hierarchical kernel cannot run keep the previous window.
* On a gfx1250 partitioned communicator, where the hierarchical AllGather can run, initialization now fails with `ncclInvalidUsage` when ranks resolve different `minCTAs` or `maxCTAs` (`NCCL_MIN_CTAS`, `NCCL_MAX_CTAS` or the communicator config), `NCCL_SYM_CTAS`, `NCCL_SYM_AG_HIER_CTAS` or `NCCL_SYM_AG_HIER_CHUNK_BYTES`. The hierarchical AllGather pairs blocks across ranks, so these mismatches would hang it.
