* On gfx1250 builds with `-DENABLE_TDM_SIMPLE=ON` and `RCCL_TDM_SIMPLE_ENABLE=1`, a Simple-protocol copy to two destinations, such as a ring AllGather step or the all-gather half of a ring AllReduce with unregistered buffers, now reads its source once and stores it to both destinations, instead of running one TDM copy per destination. On one MI450-MC node in DPX mode with 8 GPUs, 8 GB unregistered `ncclAllGather` bus bandwidth rises from 621 to 737 GB/s and `ncclAllReduce` from 685 to 740 GB/s.
