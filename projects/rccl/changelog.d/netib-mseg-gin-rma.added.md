* Multi-segment DMA-BUF registration for GIN and RMA on `IB-MSEG` with `NCCL_MULTI_SEGMENT_REGISTER=1` and `NCCL_DMABUF_ENABLE=1`: a symmetric window spanning several physical segments registers one memory region per segment, up to `RCCL_IB_REG_SEG_DEFAULT` segments within the device's `max_mr`, and RMA operations split at segment boundaries. Every rank needs the same segment count and ranks times segments is bounded; otherwise a device-memory window is left unregistered with GIN, and a GIN-only window or one with a host segment fails with `ncclInvalidUsage` on every rank. Windows with a host segment also need `NCCL_SYM_REUSE_SYSMEM_HANDLES=1`, and with the switch on the GIN and RMA proxies cap in-flight requests per peer at `RCCL_RMA_MAX_INFLIGHT_REQUESTS`.
