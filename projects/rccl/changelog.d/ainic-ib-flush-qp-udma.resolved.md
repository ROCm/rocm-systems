* Fixed lower bandwidth of the built-in `IB` transport on AINIC (for example with `NCCL_NET=IB`) since the NCCL 2.31.2 sync. The sync moved the creation of the GPUDirect RDMA flush QPs from connection setup to the first flush. The ionic driver assigns new QPs to its two uDMA pipelines alternately, so without the flush QPs between them all ring send QPs of a GPU could land on one pipeline, and large AllGather and ReduceScatter ran up to 1.4 times slower. The flush QPs are created at connection setup again.
