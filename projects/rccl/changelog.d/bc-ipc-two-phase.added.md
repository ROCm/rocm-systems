* Added `RCCL_DDA_BC_IPC_MIN_BYTES` and `RCCL_DDA_BC_IPC_MAX_BYTES`: the total-byte window in which a single-node 8-rank `ncclBroadcast` on gfx950 or gfx942 takes the two-phase DDA IPC kernel (the root scatters eighths of the message, then the ranks gather them from each other). `-1` (default) follows the per-arch table: 256 KiB-8 MiB on gfx950, 256 KiB-4 MiB on gfx942, off on gfx1250. `RCCL_DDA_BC_IPC_MAX_BYTES=0` or `RCCL_DDA_THRESHOLD=0` turns it off.
