* Fixed a multi-node gfx942 `ncclAlltoAll` bandwidth loss from 4 MiB to 256 MiB. On 2x8 MI300X, sizing each remote peer as if it owned the NIC raised the count from 2 channels per peer to 4 and ran that range at 64.5 GB/s instead of 70.9 GB/s. AlltoAll now requests 2 channels for a remote peer when the per-peer count is still above 2 after the nRanks fit. Same-node peers, Send, Recv, SendRecv and the other collectives keep their channel count. A count the fit loop has already brought to 2 or below is unchanged, and so is a user-set `NCCL_NCHANNELS_PER_NET_PEER` or `RCCL_SATURATE_P2P_NCHANNELS`. Other architectures are unaffected.
