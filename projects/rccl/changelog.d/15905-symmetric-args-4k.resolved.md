* Fixed a memory fault in symmetric collectives, such as `ncclAllReduce` on registered symmetric windows, when a group contains many operations of the same type. The symmetric scheduler filled each launch up to the 5 KB kernel-argument budget, but the symmetric kernels take a 4 KB argument, so the kernel read past its argument. Symmetric launches are now capped at 4 KB, and the scheduler returns `ncclInternalError` instead of launching a symmetric batch that does not fit.
