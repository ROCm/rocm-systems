* On gfx1250 SPX, `ncclAllReduce` defaults change with the peer-parallel DDA LL128 kernels: unset `RCCL_DDA_LL128_ONESHOT_THRESHOLD` now means 4 MiB / `nRanks` (set, it applies at every rank count), unset `RCCL_DDA_LL_THRESHOLD` now means a 1 MiB DDA LL ceiling at every rank count (it was 32 MiB, or `nRanks` x 1 MiB from 8 ranks), and from 32 ranks DDA VMM no longer goes ahead of the LL tiers between 2 MiB and `nRanks` x 512 KiB.
