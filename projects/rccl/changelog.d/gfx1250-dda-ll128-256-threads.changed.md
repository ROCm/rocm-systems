* On gfx1250 SPX comms of up to 32 ranks, the `ncclAllReduce` DDA LL128 two-shot runs 256-thread blocks (previously 512) when one pass of them covers every warp slice of the per-rank shard (up to about 2.8 MiB with the 192-block cap), so the first DDA LL128 sizes on 4, 8, 16 and 32 ranks (8 to 64 MiB) have about 20% lower latency. Larger shards, DPX and larger SPX comms keep 512 threads.
