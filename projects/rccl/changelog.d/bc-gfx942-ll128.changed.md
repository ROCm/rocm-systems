* Single-node `ncclBroadcast` on gfx942 with 3 to 8 ranks of unpartitioned GPUs uses Ring LL128 instead of Ring Simple above the Ring LL ceiling, up to 896 KiB (the 8-rank Ring Simple full grid) x the square root of the ring channel count x (ranks - 2), at most 64 MiB. Ring LL128 is offered for those Broadcasts even where the topology model leaves LL128 off, so a tuner plugin, a per-call algorithm selection naming Ring LL128 and `RCCL_OVERRIDE_PROTO=LL128` can pick it at any size.
