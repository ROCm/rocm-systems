* On gfx1250 builds with `-DENABLE_TDM_SIMPLE=ON` and `RCCL_TDM_SIMPLE_ENABLE=1`, a Simple-protocol copy to two or more destinations, such as a ring AllGather step or the all-gather half of a ring AllReduce with unregistered buffers, now reads its source once and stores it to every destination, instead of running one TDM copy per destination. The whole copy goes through the TDM mover, split evenly across the warps.
