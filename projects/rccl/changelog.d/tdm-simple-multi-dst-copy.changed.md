* On gfx1250 builds with `-DENABLE_TDM_SIMPLE=ON` and `RCCL_TDM_SIMPLE_ENABLE=1`, a Simple-protocol copy to two or more destinations, such as a ring AllGather step or the all-gather half of a ring AllReduce with unregistered buffers, now reads each tile of its source once and stores it to every destination, instead of running one TDM copy per destination.
