* On gfx1250 SPX, `ncclAllReduce` on 32 ranks (8 nodes of 4 GPUs) runs the DDA LL spread up to 4 MiB, the DDA LL two-shot up to 16 MiB and DDA LL128 up to 128 MiB, then Ring LL128 (previously DDA VMM from 2 to 16 MiB and DDA LL128 up to 256 MiB). Latency drops by 25-60% at 2-8 MiB, 12-20% at 32-64 MiB and 11% at 256 MiB. Other rank counts and DPX are unchanged.
