* gfx950 and gfx942 single-node 8-rank `ncclReduceScatter` from 64 KiB to 512 KiB runs the DDA LL one-shot in the IPC LL region on 2 to 16 blocks, with no GPU barrier and no staging copy. `RCCL_DDA_IPC_LL_THRESHOLD`, or else `RCCL_DDA_LL_THRESHOLD`, lowers the top of that window and `0` turns it off; `RCCL_DDA_LL=0` also turns it off. `RCCL_DDA_THRESHOLD` and `RCCL_IGNORE_ARCH_TABLE=1` keep it.
