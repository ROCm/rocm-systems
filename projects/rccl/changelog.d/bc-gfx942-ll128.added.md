* `RCCL_BC_SINGLE_NODE_RING_LL128_MAX_BYTES` (default `-1`): ceiling, in total message bytes, up to which single-node `ncclBroadcast` switches from Ring Simple to Ring LL128 above the Ring LL ceiling, once Ring Simple runs on every ring channel. `-1` follows the per-architecture table, `0` keeps the tuning model's protocol, and a positive value sets the ceiling on any single-node communicator of at least 2 ranks on gfx950, gfx942 or gfx1250 SPX. `NCCL_PROTO`, a tuner plugin match and a per-call algorithm selection without Ring LL128 take precedence.
