* gfx950 and gfx942 single-node 8-rank `ncclBroadcast` runs the DDA LL one-shot over the IPC scratch up to 256 KiB; `RCCL_DDA_BC=0` turns it off. Its LL banks sit in an 8 MiB region at the top of the IPC scratch, so the largest message that `RCCL_DDA_THRESHOLD` can send through the Simple DDA IPC paths drops from 256 MiB to 248 MiB. The CE-scratch copies, which use that scratch only under `NCCL_CTA_POLICY_ZERO`, have the same 248 MiB limit.
