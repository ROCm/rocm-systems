* `RCCL_HOST_HIER_ALLREDUCE_FUSED`: runs host-hierarchical AllReduce as one DDA fabric kernel that pushes each stage into peer scratch, instead of three collectives on split communicators. By default (`-1`) it is used on one or two hosts for 4 to 128 MiB `ncclSum` on FP32, FP16 or BF16; `1` uses it wherever it applies and `0` disables it. With it, the default two-host window of `RCCL_HOST_HIER_ALLREDUCE` covers 2 to 512 MiB.
