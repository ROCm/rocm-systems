* `RCCL_HOST_HIER_ALLREDUCE_FUSED`: runs host-hierarchical AllReduce as one DDA fabric kernel that pushes each stage into peer scratch, instead of three collectives on split communicators. By default (`-1`) it is used on one or two hosts with eight ranks per host for 4 to 16 MiB `ncclSum` on FP32, FP16 or BF16; `1` uses it wherever it applies and `0` disables it. `RCCL_DDA_ENABLE=0` also disables it.
