* On gfx950 and gfx942, single-node 8-rank DDA IPC `ncclAllReduce`, `ncclAllGather`, `ncclReduceScatter` and `ncclAllToAll` calls whose kernel copies the input into the DDA scratch alternate between two 8 MiB scratch banks and skip the barrier at the end of the kernel. The rest of the DDA scratch, and so the largest copy-engine scratch transfer, shrinks by 16 MiB. `RCCL_DDA_IPC_BANKED_SCRATCH` (default `-1`, follow the arch table: on for gfx950 and gfx942) set to `0` turns it off, and any other non-zero value turns it on.
