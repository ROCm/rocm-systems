* Multi-segment DMA-BUF registration for NET transfers on `IB-MSEG` with `NCCL_MULTI_SEGMENT_REGISTER=1`: a send or receive buffer spanning several physical segments registers one memory region per segment, up to the build-time limit `RCCL_IB_REG_SEG_DEFAULT` within the device's `max_mr`, and uses GPUDirect RDMA instead of staging; GIN and port-failover connections still stage it. When the two ends of a connection reach different multi-segment decisions from `NCCL_MULTI_SEGMENT_REGISTER`, `NCCL_DMABUF_ENABLE` or `NCCL_IB_RESILIENCY_PORT_FAILOVER`, the accepting rank fails with `ncclInvalidUsage` and its peer sees the connection close. Per multi-segment connection, each side's 128 KiB CTS FIFO becomes a 512 KiB slot array (about 384 KiB more per side) and the send side adds about 11 KiB of segmented-send state.
