* `RCCL_BC_SINGLE_NODE_RING_LL_MAX_BYTES` (default `-1`): ceiling, in total message bytes, up to which single-node `ncclBroadcast` switches from Ring Simple to Ring LL. `-1` follows the per-architecture table, `0` keeps the tuning model's protocol, and a positive value sets the ceiling at any rank count. The default table covers gfx950 and gfx942 at 2 to 8 ranks on unpartitioned GPUs, and most comms with ranks on partitioned GPUs keep the tuning model; a positive value also applies on gfx1250 SPX, past 8 ranks and on partitioned GPUs. gfx1250 DPX uses its own Broadcast ceilings. `NCCL_PROTO`, a tuner plugin match and a per-call algorithm selection without Ring LL take precedence.
