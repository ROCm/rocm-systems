* On gfx1250, a single-node FP8 e4m3 or e5m2 `ncclAllReduce` with more than 8 ranks no longer gets the 1024x ring penalty at any size when DDA LL128 is enabled, not only at or above the DDA LL128 ceiling. Sizes that DDA declines, such as byte counts not divisible by the rank count, can now use Ring. The penalty stays when the DDA LL128 ceiling is zero and for every other FP8 ring above 8 ranks.
