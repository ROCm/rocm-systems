* On gfx1250 SPX, AllToAll takes the DDA tiers that DPX takes: DDA LL up to 128 KiB total (was 64 KiB), DDA LL128 above it up to 16 MiB (was 8 MiB), and the DDA fabric VMM push above that up to 256 MiB (the SPX table left the VMM tier off). Larger messages take per-peer Send/Recv as before. `RCCL_DDA_LL_THRESHOLD`, `RCCL_DDA_LL128_THRESHOLD` and `RCCL_DDA_THRESHOLD` still override the table.
