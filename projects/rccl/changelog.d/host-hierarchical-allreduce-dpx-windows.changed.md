* `RCCL_HOST_HIER_ALLREDUCE`: the default (`-1`) window on two hosts with eight ranks per host (DPX) is now 2 to 16 MiB and above 64 MiB up to 128 MiB (was 2 to 512 MiB). On gfx1250, host-hierarchical AllReduce was 9% slower than the default path at 32 MiB and even with it at 64 MiB, the three collectives were 4% slower at 256 MiB, and 512 MiB was within noise.
