* `rccl_ep` C entry points check their arguments and fail with one `[rccl_ep] <fn>: <reason>` line before launching anything or changing any state, and `ep_create` fails on every rank of the group when any rank's device is not wave64. The standalone `rccl_ep` build compiles gfx9 targets only, and configuring with `ENABLE_RCCL_EP_TESTS` but without `ENABLE_MPI_TESTS` is an error instead of silently building no `rccl_ep` tests. For the MPI tests, the test-only hooks `RCCL_EP_FAKE_WAVE_SIZE`, `RCCL_EP_FAKE_WAVE_SIZE_RANK` and `RCCL_EP_FAKE_ALLOC_FAIL_RANK` fake a non-wave64 device or a failed scratch allocation on one rank.
