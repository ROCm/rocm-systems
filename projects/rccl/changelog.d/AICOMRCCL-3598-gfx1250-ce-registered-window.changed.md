* On gfx1250, registered (`-R 2`) AllReduce and AllGather no longer take the CE-registered path where symk or Ring is faster, in SPX and DPX. AllReduce keeps CE-registered up to 4 MiB, for ops that symk and DDA cannot run, then runs symk up to a per-rank element cap that grows with the communicator to at most 32 MiB, and Ring above it. AllGather keeps CE-registered only up to 32 MiB in SPX, where it serves types DDA cannot run, and no longer uses it in DPX. `CTAPolicy=ZERO` still selects CE-registered at every size, and `RCCL_CE_AR_REG_MAX_MSG_BYTES`, `RCCL_CE_COLL_MAX_BYTES` and `RCCL_SYM_K_MAX_BYTES` still override the table.
