* On gfx1250, Ring Simple drains each step's loads and stores once in a fused end-of-step barrier, its start-of-step barrier orders LDS only, and `reduceCopy` stores 16-byte packs as buffer stores. The CMake options `ENABLE_SIMPLE_FUSED_STEP_END`, `ENABLE_SIMPLE_STORE_FREE_STEP_START` and `ENABLE_SIMPLE_BUFFER_STORE` (default `ON`) restore the previous behavior when set to `OFF`.
