* On gfx1250, Ring Simple drains each step's loads and stores once in a fused end-of-step barrier, its start-of-step barrier orders LDS only, and `reduceCopy` stores 16-byte packs as buffer stores. SPX Ring AllReduce keeps LL128 up to nRanks x 4 MiB, then only where a per-step chunk cost model prices it under Simple. The CMake options `ENABLE_SIMPLE_FUSED_STEP_END`, `ENABLE_SIMPLE_STORE_FREE_STEP_START`, `ENABLE_SIMPLE_BUFFER_STORE` and `ENABLE_GFX1250_AR_RING_LL128_BY_CHUNK` (default `ON`) restore the previous behavior when set to `OFF`.
