* Fixed profiler plugins with kernel-channel (`KernelCh`) events enabled getting extra kernel-channel operations for point-to-point tasks on channels 64 and above. The host walked every channel up to `MAXCHANNELS` and shifted the 64-bit P2P channel mask by the channel index, which is undefined from 64 on and wraps on x86. Each P2P channel `c` therefore also posted an operation on channels `c + 64`, `c + 128` and `c + 192`, and advanced their work counters. The device never completes those operations, and the advanced counters no longer match the device's counters for later work on those channels.
