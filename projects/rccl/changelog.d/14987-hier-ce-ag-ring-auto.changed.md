* `NCCL_HIER_CE_COLL_AG_RAIL_RING_ENABLE` (default `-1`): the default now selects the inter-node rail algorithm of hierarchical Copy Engine `ncclAllGather` automatically instead of always using the direct path: direct on two nodes, and on three or more nodes ring once each rank contributes at least `NCCL_HIER_CE_COLL_AG_RAIL_RING_THRESHOLD` bytes. A positive value forces the ring and `0` forces the direct path, the previous default.
