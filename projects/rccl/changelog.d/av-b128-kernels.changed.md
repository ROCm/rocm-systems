* RCCL's kernels now use `RCCL_LOAD_B128_SYS` / `RCCL_STORE_B128_SYS` for their system-scope 128-bit loads and stores, and `RCCL_HAVE_SYS_B128` for the paths that depend on them, instead of `__builtin_amdgcn_global_load_b128` / `global_store_b128` and `RCCL_HAVE_GLOBAL_DWORDX4_BUILTINS`. The 128-bit paths, including the gfx1250 LL and LL128 comm-FIFO fix (`RCCL_LL_FIFO_SYS_SCOPE`) and the gfx1250 Ring Simple store-free step start and buffer stores, therefore stay enabled with compilers that provide only `__builtin_amdgcn_av_load_b128` / `av_store_b128`. With compilers that provide both, the generated code is unchanged.
