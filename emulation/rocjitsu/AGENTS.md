# Commit messages

When creating or amending a commit, use a concise imperative subject followed
by exactly two body paragraphs. The first explains the problem or motivation;
the second describes the final technical mechanism and relevant verification.
Describe only the committed change in human-readable terms, without
intermediate fixes, review history, or planning references. Wrap lines at
about 80 characters, preferably no more than 80. Avoid subject-only messages
unless the change is genuinely trivial.

Do not sign commits in this checkout unless the user explicitly requests it.
